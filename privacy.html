Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Navi Solutions LLC ("Navi," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information in connection with (a) the website located at navisolutions.ai (the "Website"), and (b) the software applications that Navi operates for internal business purposes, including the application known as "Navi Cost Agent" (each, an "Application"). By accessing the Website or authorizing an Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an inquiry through the Website's contact form, schedule a consultation, or correspond with us by email, we collect the information you elect to provide, which may include your name, email address, company name, and the content of your message. You should not submit sensitive personal information, including health information, financial account numbers, or government-issued identification numbers, through the Website.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, our hosting and service providers may automatically collect limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages viewed. Navi uses aggregate, cookie-free analytics and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking technologies.

2.3 Information Obtained Through an Application

The Applications are not offered to the public and are used solely by authorized Navi personnel. Upon the express authorization of an account holder, an Application may access the following data:

  • Google user data. Read-only access to Gmail (scope: gmail.readonly) to identify billing receipts and invoices from designated vendors, and access to Google Drive limited to files and folders created by the Application (scope: drive.file) to store such records and an associated cost-tracking spreadsheet. The Application cannot access any other content in the user's Google Drive.
  • Microsoft 365 data. Read-only access (permission: Mail.Read) to mailboxes for which a client has authorized Navi access in connection with an engagement, limited to identifying billing receipts and invoices from designated vendors.

The Applications process only those messages that match the designated vendors, extract the transaction amount, date, and reference number, and retain a copy of the corresponding receipt. The Applications do not send, modify, or delete email messages.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to: (a) respond to inquiries and schedule consultations; (b) perform services under a written agreement with a client, including recording and reporting the costs associated with a client engagement; (c) operate, maintain, and secure the Website and the Applications; and (d) comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

4. Google API Services: Limited Use Disclosure

Navi's use and transfer to any other application of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Without limiting the foregoing, Navi does not: (a) use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or personalized or interest-based advertising; (b) sell Google user data; (c) use Google user data to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models; or (d) permit any person to read Google user data except with the user's affirmative consent, where necessary for security purposes or to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

5. Disclosure of Information

Navi does not sell or rent personal information. We disclose information only as follows:

  • Clients. Records relating to a client engagement are disclosed only to the client to whom they pertain, through a Google Drive folder shared with that client.
  • Service providers. We engage the following providers to operate the Website, each of which processes information subject to its own privacy policy: Cloudflare, Inc. (cookie-free web analytics); Calendly LLC (appointment scheduling); Web3Forms (contact form delivery); Google LLC (web font delivery, which transmits your IP address to Google); and GitHub, Inc. (website hosting).
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information where required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Navi, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of Navi's assets, subject to the commitments in this Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Navi does not place its own cookies on the Website. The embedded scheduling service provided by Calendly may place cookies in accordance with its own policies. You may block or delete cookies through your browser settings; however, doing so may affect the functionality of the embedded scheduler.

7. Data Security

Navi maintains reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Application authorization tokens are stored only on Navi-controlled devices, and access to engagement records is restricted to authorized Navi personnel. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Navi cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain inquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and follow up. We retain engagement and financial records for the duration of the applicable engagement and thereafter for the period required to satisfy tax, accounting, and legal obligations, after which such records are deleted.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information that Navi holds about you, and you may opt out of further communications from us. Residents of California and other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws, and individuals in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, may have additional rights, including the right to data portability and the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Navi will respond to verifiable requests as required by applicable law and will not discriminate against any individual for exercising these rights. To submit a request, contact us using the information in Section 13.

Any account holder who has authorized an Application may revoke that authorization at any time through the security settings of their Google Account (Third-party connections) or through myapps.microsoft.com.
