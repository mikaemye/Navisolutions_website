Co-founder · Navi Solutions

Mikaela Myers

Mikaela Myers is Co-Founder of Navi Solutions, where she designs and builds AI-powered systems, automation tools, and agentic workflows that help organizations adopt AI responsibly, effectively, and at scale.

Before founding Navi, Mikaela spent nearly eight years at Amazon and AWS, building deep expertise at the intersection of artificial intelligence, product strategy, and responsible innovation. Most recently, she served as a Senior Technical Product Manager focused on Responsible AI, leading initiatives that embedded responsible AI best practices across traditional machine learning, generative AI, and agentic systems throughout the AWS stack.

Her experience spans responsible AI frameworks, model governance, gold-standard dataset development, and tooling for teams working in computer vision, multimodal AI, generative AI, and agent architectures. In 2025, she announced the launch of the AWS Responsible AI Best Practice Framework at AWS re:Invent and co-led product development for a model customization agent within SageMaker AI.

Earlier in her career, Mikaela was a product manager for multiple image-based models for Amazon Visual Search and led large-scale fulfillment center operations, experiences that shaped her ability to bridge technical innovation with operational execution.

Having traveled to more than forty countries, Mikaela brings a global perspective and deep curiosity about how people learn, work, and adapt to change. That curiosity continues to fuel her work as an entrepreneur, helping organizations navigate one of the most transformative technological shifts of our time.

Co-founder · Navi Solutions

Sean Myers

Sean Myers is defined by six words: consistency, forward thinking, fun, curiosity, execution, and navigation.

A lifelong entrepreneur and a co-founder of Navi Solutions, Sean has spent many decades navigating technology’s most pivotal shifts. From the client-server revolution of the 1980s and ‘90s, through the dot-com era, the rise of ASP (Application Service Provider) platforms that foreshadowed modern SaaS, and into today’s cloud and AI-driven world. As an early-stage founder and investor in infrastructure and deep technology, he has built and led large, high-performing multinational teams, turning bold ideas into operating businesses across borders and time zones. Sean has always had a knack for seeing around the corner. He began investing in the ASP space before the internet bubble even popped, recognizing the future of software delivery long before the market caught up.

That spirit of navigation runs deeper than technology. When his daughter Mikaela was ten years old, Sean moved the family aboard a sailboat and spent fifteen months sailing the world’s oceans, from the British Virgin Islands to Australia. That adventure captures something essential about who Sean is: that the best journeys, in business and in life, require equal parts preparation, courage, and a genuine love for the ride.
